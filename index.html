About Beck's Junk Hauling & Removal

Scott Beck is the owner of Beck’s Junk Hauling and Removal, based in Lake Monticello. He started the business while assisting with real estate cleanouts and quickly recognized the need for honest, reliable junk and yard debris removal services. As a dedicated husband and father of four, Scott proudly serves homeowners and surrounding areas with dependable, no-hassle service.
